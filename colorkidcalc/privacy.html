Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Color Kid Calc ("Renk Ustasi", "the App") is developed by Gokhan Alpatar ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Color Kid Calc is designed with children's privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Purchase History: When you make in-app purchases, Apple processes the transaction. We receive purchase verification data to unlock premium features. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • Local Data: Game progress, scores, achievements, credits, and preferences are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, address, phone number)
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles
  • We do not share any data with third parties

3. Advertising

The free version of the App may display banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. For users under the age of consent, ads are served in a child-directed manner in compliance with COPPA and applicable regulations. Premium users do not see any advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Color Kid Calc is designed for children aged 4 and older. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • The App includes a parental gate for purchases and account actions
  • No social features, chat, or user-generated content
  • No behavioral advertising targeted at children

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions and consumable coin packs). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. A parental gate is implemented to prevent unauthorized purchases.

6. Data Storage and Security

All user data (game progress, preferences, achievements) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers or databases that store user data. If you delete the App, all local data is permanently removed.

7. Delete Account / Data

You can delete all your data at any time through the App's Profile section by tapping "Delete Account". This permanently removes all game progress, credits, achievements, and preferences from your device.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: For in-app purchase processing
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (free version only)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
