EN: CleanGuard Ultra does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. Specifically:

  • Photos & Videos: Accessed only on-device to find duplicates, blurry shots, large files and sensitive content. Never uploaded. Analysis happens locally using on-device frameworks.
  • Files & Storage: Scanned locally to report storage usage. No file content is transmitted.
  • No Account: The app has no sign-up, login, or user profile.
  • Purchases: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Diagnostics: Optional, anonymized crash data may be provided by the platform (Apple/Google) to help us fix bugs. This contains no personal content.
