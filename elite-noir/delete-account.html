Delete Account

We respect your right to delete your account and all associated data.

⚠ Before You Delete

Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. The following will be permanently removed:

  • Your membership status and prestige progress
  • All saved wallpapers and preferences
  • Wealth calculator data
  • Purchase proof and member ID
  • All locally stored app data

Note: Deleting your account does NOT automatically cancel active subscriptions. You must cancel subscriptions separately (see below).

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In the App

1

Open ÉLITE NOIR app

2

Go to Profile tab (bottom right)

3

Scroll down and tap "Delete Account & Data"

4

Confirm deletion when prompted

5

All local data is immediately and permanently erased

Option 2: Via Email

Send an email to delete@elitenoirapp.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will process your request within 48 hours.

Option 3: Uninstall the App

Since all data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the App will permanently delete all associated data.

Cancel Subscriptions

iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → ÉLITE NOIR → Cancel

Android: Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → ÉLITE NOIR → Cancel

Subscriptions must be cancelled at least 24 hours before the next renewal date.

What Happens After Deletion

  • All local app data is permanently erased
  • Your prestige progress resets to zero
  • Your member ID becomes invalid
  • Downloaded wallpapers saved outside the app are not affected
  • If you reinstall the app, you start fresh with no prior data
  • Your app purchase ($999.99) remains valid — you can re-download without repurchasing

Contact

Questions about account deletion? Contact us at support@elitenoirapp.com
