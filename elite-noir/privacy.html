Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 28, 2026

1. Introduction

ÉLITE NOIR ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ÉLITE NOIR (the "App"), available on iOS and Android platforms.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: We do not require account creation. The App functions without registration.
  • Purchase Information: In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, credit card numbers, or billing details.
  • Wealth Calculator Data: Any financial data you enter in the Wealth Calculator is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality purposes.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, feature usage, and session duration to improve the App experience.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do NOT collect location data.
  • We do NOT collect photos, contacts, or any data from other apps.
  • We do NOT collect financial or banking information.
  • We do NOT use cookies or web tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Process and verify in-app subscription status (via Apple/Google APIs)
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage

All user-generated data (wealth calculator entries, preferences, settings) is stored locally on your device using the device's native storage. This data is not transmitted to any external server.

Subscription status is managed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play Store infrastructure.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit 2: For processing iOS in-app purchases and subscriptions. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: For processing Android in-app purchases and subscriptions. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no data to retain. Local data on your device persists until you delete the App or clear app data.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your data: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data. You can also delete your account and data through the App's Settings page or our Account Deletion page.
  • Cancel subscriptions: Manage subscriptions through your device's App Store or Google Play Store settings.
  • Request information: Contact us to inquire about any data we may have.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 17. The App's content and pricing are designed exclusively for adult users.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect any information processed by the App. Since the App operates primarily offline with local storage, the attack surface is minimal.

11. International Users

The App is available worldwide. By using the App, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy, regardless of your country of residence.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
