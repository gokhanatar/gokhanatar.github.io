Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unsaid Room ("we", "us", the "App") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email address and password (managed by Firebase Authentication), display name, and profile details you provide.
  • Profile photos: images you choose from your camera or photo library, used only for your profile.
  • User-generated content: community questions, chat/direct messages, feed posts, and in-game answers you create.
  • Purchase data: subscription and in-app purchase records, verified through Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing. We never receive your full payment card details.
  • Usage data: game progress, achievements, and preferences needed to run the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the multiplayer game, social features, and your account.
  • To deliver in-app purchases and premium features.
  • To send push notifications you have opted into.
  • To keep the community safe and enforce our Terms of Use.

3. On-Device Translation

When a card is not available in your language, the App may translate it entirely on your device using Google ML Kit. This translation happens offline; the text is not sent to our servers or any third party for translation.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, cloud functions, push) — Firebase Privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — for purchase processing.
  • Google ML Kit — on-device translation only.

We do not use advertising trackers and do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. User-Generated Content & Safety

The App is intended for adults (18+) and contains user-generated content. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. You can report content or users, and block other users, at any time. Reported content is reviewed and acted upon within 24 hours, and offending users may be removed.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Settings → Account), which removes your personal data from our systems. Some records may be retained where required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, KVKK), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
