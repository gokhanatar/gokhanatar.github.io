CringeVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and profile photo (if provided via Google or Apple sign-in). We also store your cringe vault entries, court submissions, votes, and app preferences in our secure database (Firebase/Firestore).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the CringeVault service. Vault entries are private and visible only to you. Court submissions are displayed anonymously. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Google Firebase servers with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication with support for email/password, Google, and Apple sign-in.

4. Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and all associated data, export your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Use the in-app Settings page or contact us at privacy@cringevault.app.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store your language preference, theme selection, and sound settings. Firebase may use cookies for authentication. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

7. Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send you alerts when your vault entries unlock, court submissions reach milestones, or comments are posted. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

CringeVault is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@cringevault.app
