Overview

Metabolica ("we", "our", "the app") is a TDEE and nutrition tracking application published by Gökhan Alp Atar. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Metabolica operates fully offline. All personal data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us or any external server:

  • Body metrics you enter (weight, height, age, sex, activity level)
  • Food log entries and meal data
  • Body measurements and progress photos (stored only on-device)
  • Goal settings and progress history
  • App preferences (language, theme, reminders)

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. There is no account or login.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing — Processes subscription and purchase transactions. Payment data is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment details.
  • Capacitor Local Notifications — Delivers on-device reminders. No data leaves your device.

Subscriptions

Metabolica offers Monthly and Yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime purchase. All transactions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. See our Terms of Use and EULA for details.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app; uninstalling permanently erases all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Metabolica is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
