Privacy Policy

Overview

Metabolica ("we", "our", "the app") is a TDEE and nutrition tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Metabolica operates fully offline. All personal data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Body metrics you enter (weight, height, age, activity level)
  • Food log entries and meal data
  • Body measurements (waist, hip, chest, arm, thigh, neck)
  • Goal settings and progress data
  • App preferences and theme settings

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Third-Party SDKs

The app uses the following third-party services which may collect non-personal, aggregated data:

  • Google AdMob — Displays advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing — Processes subscription purchases. Payment data is handled entirely by Apple or Google.
  • Capacitor Local Notifications — Sends local reminders. No data leaves the device.

Advertising

Metabolica displays ads to free users via Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Subscriptions

Metabolica offers weekly and monthly premium subscriptions. All purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by:

  • Using the "Delete Account" option in Profile → Settings
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Metabolica is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
