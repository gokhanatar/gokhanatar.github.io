Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

CleanGuard Ultra ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how CleanGuard Ultra handles information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

CleanGuard Ultra does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All operations are performed entirely on your device.

Data Processing

The app accesses the following device data solely to provide its features:

  • Photos and Media: To analyze, organize, and compress media files. All processing occurs locally on your device.
  • Contacts: To detect duplicates and incomplete entries. Contact data is never uploaded or shared.
  • Calendar: To identify and remove spam calendar events. Calendar data stays on your device.
  • Storage: To analyze storage usage and clean cache files.
  • Network: To perform speed tests and scan connected devices. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Notifications: To send optional maintenance reminders.

Third-Party Services

CleanGuard Ultra uses the following third-party services for core functionality:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing: For subscription management. Subject to Apple/Google privacy policies.

Data Security

Since no personal data leaves your device, there is no risk of data breach on our servers. All sensitive operations use on-device processing only.

Children's Privacy

CleanGuard Ultra is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Any changes will be reflected by an updated date above.
